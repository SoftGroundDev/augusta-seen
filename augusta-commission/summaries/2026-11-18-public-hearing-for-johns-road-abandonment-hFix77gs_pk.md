## TL;DR

* This was a public hearing on abandoning the section of Johns Road between Gardner Street and Fitton Street next to the cemetery and Augusta Country Club. The presiding officer said no decision would be made; comments were recorded for the commission's later consideration [00:07:54] [00:22:25].
* Supporters, led by Matt Mills, who spoke for Augusta Country Club, cited driving range expansion, less road for the county to maintain, a new cul-de-sac, cemetery parking, sidewalk and landscaping, and less cut-through traffic.
