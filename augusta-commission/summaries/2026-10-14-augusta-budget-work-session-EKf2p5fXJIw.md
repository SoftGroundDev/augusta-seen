### Courts overview (Administrator Allen)

* The courts include 11 offices, from Superior Court and the district attorney to probate court and the public defender. Slides showed five years of actuals, the 2026 budget with year-to-date spending, and the 2027 recommendation [00:16:23] [00:16:36].
* "Court costs are people costs": salaries and benefits were 77% of 2025 spending and 81% of the 2027 recommendation [00:16:47]. Court spending grows about $6.1 million from 2024 actual to the 2027 recommendation, and $6 million of that is personnel [00:17:06].
* Court costs were $19.7 million in 2021 and $24.2 million in 2025, up 23% [00:17:32]. Revenue (fines, fees, and partner county reimbursements) stayed between $5.8 million and $7 million a year. It covered about 36% of costs in 2021 and 27% in 2025, and the general fund covers the difference [00:17:43] [00:18:13].
* The 2027 recommendation for all court functions is $28 million in expenses and $8.3 million in revenue [00:18:31]. Personnel grew from $16.6 million in 2024 to $22.6 million, and regular full-time salaries from $12 million to $16.4 million [00:18:48].
* In 2027, more employee health insurance cost is charged directly to department budgets instead of at the general fund level, so each court budget rises [00:19:07]. Non-personnel costs (contracts, juror fees, rent, supplies) stayed between $4.9 million and $5.6 million [00:19:35].
* From 2021 to 2025, court costs grew $4.45 million. The district attorney accounted for $1.63 million, almost all in 2025, when approved pay increases took effect and salaries and benefits rose $1.25 million [00:20:03]. The solicitor general, juvenile court, Superior Court and circuit, and state court each grew about $390,000 to $470,000 [00:20:42].
