## TL;DR

* After executive session, the commission voted by roll call to accept Administrator Tamika Allen's resignation to retire effective June 30, 2027, with 10 years of retirement credit and one year of salary continuation as severance. It carried with Ms. Pulliam absent. Allen has served the city more than 34 years. A LiDAR equipment purchase, contingent on a Governor's Office of Planning and Budget grant, also passed.
* Public Services voted (clerk: "unanimous") to have the administrator issue an RFP for a broker to sell surplus parks from the ISM divestment list. A.L. Williams, Dowdy, and Vernon Forest parks were taken off the list.
* The new CSRA Short-Term Rental Alliance (Deanna Schaefer) asked the commission to reject the published zoning rewrite draft, clarify the timeline, and give the group a seat on a working group. Planning Director Cameron said the STR section has been pulled and is still being drafted, that Masters rentals of 14 days or less are not affected, and that it may reach the Planning Commission around December 7. The delegation was received as information.
* Finance Committee sent the FY2027 budget to the full commission for a vote on Tuesday, November 17 (carried with Ms. Pulliam out). About $190,870 in late changes come out of contingency. Restoring transit routes 5, 6, and 7 would cost $385,160 and is not in the budget.
* Engineering Services and Administrative Services consent agendas passed. Public Safety lacked a quorum. Commissioner Wimberly pressed for a clear grounds management plan before the November 12 work session, and new Grounds Maintenance Director Roger Garvin was recognized.

### Item 3: Divestment of underutilized parks (Director Williams, Recreation and Parks)

* Infrastructure Systems Management (ISM) evaluated parks starting in September 2025, with a year of further analysis through September 2026 [03:10:05].
* Facilities scoring 20 or below were reviewed. Costs cover contract services, labor, landscaping, equipment, repairs, infrastructure, and amenities [03:11:26].
* Recommended divestment sites: A.L. Williams, Alexander Barrett, Bedford Heights, Dowdy, Elliott, Hurd Avenue, Valley, Vernon Forest, and W.T. Johnson [03:12:38].
* Selected park figures as presented (estimated cost; average monthly visits): A.L. Williams, 0.9 acres, $33,000 [03:13:12]; Bedford Heights, 1.87 acres, about $39,000 [03:13:48]; Dowdy (park and center), 2.79 acres, $107,000 [03:14:05]; Elliott, 0.96 acres, $61,000, about 12 visits [03:14:25]; Hurd Avenue, 1.14 acres, about $22,000 [03:14:38]; Valley, 1.52 acres, about $43,000, about 6 visitors [03:14:55]; Vernon Forest, 1.43 acres, about $50,000, about 8 visitors [03:15:16]; W.T. * Scoring used classification, condition, distance to the nearest park (0.5 to over 1 mile), acreage, usage score, and average monthly visitation. None of the sites had funding set aside [03:16:17].
* Tiered model: Tier 1 retain and invest; Tier 2 retain and optimize; Tier 3 repurpose, consolidate, or partner, including shared-use agreements or leases with nonprofits and community partners; Tier 4 divest or dispose through transfer, long-term lease, third-party operation, sale, or conversion to passive green space [03:18:01].
* Recommendation: divest underutilized parks and redirect resources to remaining parks, or allocate more maintenance funding [03:21:28].
* Commissioner Scott asked to remove Vernon Forest, which a group (transcribed "term back the block") is about to take over, and Dowdy, because of nearby housing authority apartment construction [03:22:50]. Williams confirmed an approved RFP and work with the Vernon Forest group [03:23:21].

### Item 5: CSRA Short-Term Rental Alliance (delegation, Deanna Schaefer)

* Schaefer, a Richmond County resident in Country Club Hills, founded the CSRA Short-Term Rental Alliance, a coalition of owners, hosts, managers, and local businesses seeking balanced STR policy [03:34:29] [03:36:20].
* Her examples included deployed military homeowners, families with babies in the NICU, and medical school interviewees [03:38:03].
* She said Airbnb collected and remitted over $1 million in the 8% hotel-motel tax in the past year and over $5 million since inception [03:39:04]. 311 data showed five nuisance reports in five years, and Airbnb reports complaints on under 0.1% of reservations [03:40:02].
* Proposals: a simple, affordable registration or permit system; a local representative for non-local owners; a required education course; and annual fees to fund enforcement staff [03:42:04].
* Requests: reject the "ZA 263" zoning ordinance rewrite in its prior and current published form; clarify the status and timeline; include the alliance in a steering committee or working group, or create one [03:43:13].
* Planning Director Cameron said the STR framework shown at the October 21 public hearing (as she recalled the date) has been removed, the draft is online, and staff are working with Administrator Allen and committee members [03:45:02]. The finished document goes to the Planning Commission, then the commission, then the public [03:46:10].
* Cameron said Masters rentals are not affected: the federal "Augusta rule" allows renting up to 14 days untaxed [03:46:32]. STR provisions will not go before the Planning Commission until, she believed, December 7 [03:54:52].
* Guilfoyle said he prefers taking time to get it right and cited a District 8 problem property on Rawlins Road [03:47:18] [03:49:04].
* Commissioner Clark called the "Augusta getting rid of Masters" headline disingenuous [03:51:59]. He read an Airbnb email to hosts saying an October 14 draft would have banned STRs in most districts, required permits, and capped occupancy at two adults per bedroom up to six, and naming the alliance [03:56:13].
* Commissioner Slendak said she has six STRs on her street, that many problems are never reported to 311 because it does not respond until Monday, and that some operators bypass Airbnb entirely [03:59:21].
* Schaefer said the current draft simply reads "reserved," leaving stakeholders "in the dark" [04:02:37]. Cameron said Schaefer's input will be brought to a meeting with the administrator next week [04:03:31].
* Mayor Johnson objected to investors turning single-family houses into year-round event venues and asked what that does to hoteliers [04:05:20]. Schaefer owns one STR, operates others full time, and offered to teach a required good-neighbor course [04:07:35] [04:09:21].
* Commissioner Jordan Johnson said Augusta lags other cities, citing limits on downtown food trucks [04:12:43]. Cameron said the final product will include mixed-use zoning, not only residential [04:14:11].
* Commissioner Wimberly distinguished homeowners renting during the Masters from investors running STRs as businesses; Cameron agreed [04:15:24]. Cameron said STRs register with the city as businesses [04:18:25].
