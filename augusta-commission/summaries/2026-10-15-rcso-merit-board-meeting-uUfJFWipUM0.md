## TL;DR

* The RCSO Merit Board heard the appeal of former deputy Calvin Swan, terminated on or about September 3, 2026. The sheriff's office said he submitted special duty hours he did not work. At the end, the chair announced that the board would uphold the sheriff's termination. No motion or roll call vote appears in the transcript [00:10:51] [01:58:00].
* Sheriff's witnesses (Lt. James Kelly, Sgt.
