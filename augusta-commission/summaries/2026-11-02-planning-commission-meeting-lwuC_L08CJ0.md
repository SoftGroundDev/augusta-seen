### Item 6: ZA-26-4, vape shop text amendment

* Ebony Dobson of Planning and Development presented the case. The amendment changes Section 26-1 of the comprehensive zoning ordinance. Vape shops would no longer be a by-right use and would need special exception or special use permit approval [00:10:18] [00:10:37].
* The definition covers retail and wholesale sellers of vaping products, whether or not the products contain nicotine. A store with other main products counts as a vape shop only if at least 10% of its retail floor area is devoted to vapor products [00:12:00] [00:12:10].
* Key provisions [00:12:31] [00:13:01] [00:13:24]:
  * SUP approval in designated districts.
  * A 5,000-foot separation from existing or approved vape shops.
  * A 5,000-foot separation from schools, child care facilities, places of worship, and public parks and recreation facilities.
  * Distance measured in a straight line between property boundaries.
  * Applications must include site and floor plans, operating information, and maps of nearby vape shops and protected uses.
  * Existing lawful shops can continue as nonconforming uses. Conversions, relocations, and certain expansions need new approval.
* Commissioner McKnight asked whether the change would allow more sales of unregulated kratom, citing reported illnesses and deaths [00:14:03]. A staff member (not named) said the amendment restricts vape shops rather than opening the door. New shops would have to come before the Planning Commission and the Augusta Commission and meet strict distance rules [00:15:04].
* Staff said an incoming business posted illegal signs, which led Commissioner Smith Rice to ask for a count. That count found about 92 shops. Working with the sheriff and marshal raised it to about 400, so staff called the city "saturated" [00:15:40] [00:15:55].
* A "Commissioner Smith" asked what the city is doing about existing shops [00:16:40]. Staff said code enforcement is checking each shop's business license against what it actually sells. Shops that are not selling what they reported would come under the amendment, and staff agreed there would be punitive measures [00:17:11] [00:17:31].
* No one in the audience spoke for or against the amendment [00:17:49]. The motion to approve carried [00:18:26].
* Because the change amends the code, it needs two readings. General counsel will handle that [00:18:31] [00:18:41].
